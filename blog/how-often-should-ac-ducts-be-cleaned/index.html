How Often Should AC Ducts Be Cleaned in the UAE?

4 November 2026 · 3 min read · By the Tip Top team

How often should AC ducts be cleaned in the UAE? For most homes with the AC running much of the year, about once a year is a sensible baseline, ideally before the hottest months. Homes with pets, allergies, nearby construction or a lot of dust may need it more often, while a lightly used system with a well-kept filter may manage a little longer. The right interval depends on your home, and the signs in your system will tell you.

Why UAE homes need it more often than you might think

In many countries, the AC runs for a few months a year. In the UAE it often runs for most of the year, so far more air, and far more dust, passes through the ducts. Fine sand and dust get indoors easily, and high humidity keeps the coil and drain tray damp. All of that adds up to faster build-up than general advice from cooler climates assumes.

How often should AC ducts be cleaned? A practical guide

Your homeHow often to do AC duct cleaning
Apartment or villa, AC used most of the year, no petsAbout once a year
Dogs or cats in the homeConsider more often than once a year
Someone with allergies or asthmaAsk for an inspection; often more than once a year
Near construction, a busy road or open sandMore often, guided by how quickly dust returns
Smokers in the homeMore often, as smoke residue builds up in ducts
Holiday home or lightly used AC, clean filterPossibly less often; check the system yearly
After renovation or a heavy dust stormAs soon as the work or storm is over
Moving into a new homeBefore moving in, if possible

These are practical guidelines, not rules. If your building management or landlord has its own maintenance schedule, follow that.

Signs you need it sooner

  • Dust settles on surfaces near the vents within a day or two of cleaning.
  • Dark streaks appear around ceiling grilles.
  • A musty smell when the AC starts.
  • Some rooms cool less well than others.
  • Visible mould on grilles or the indoor unit.
  • Indoor allergy symptoms that ease when you go out. These can have many causes, so see a doctor if they persist.

Our article on why your AC smells can help you work out whether a smell is coming from the ducts or somewhere else.

What to clean, and how often

Duct cleaning is only one part of the routine. Different parts of the system need attention at different intervals.

PartWhoSuggested frequency
FilterYouEvery few weeks during heavy use; more often in dusty spells
Grilles and vents (outside surface)YouWipe when you see dust, roughly monthly
Coil and drain trayProfessionalAt least once a year, often with the duct clean
Inside the ductsProfessionalAbout once a year, or as above
Mechanical serviceAC technicianAs recommended for your unit, or when cooling drops

AC vent cleaning, how often? Wiping the outside of grilles is a quick monthly job you can do yourself with the AC off. It keeps vents looking clean, but it doesn't reach the dust inside the ducts, so it doesn't replace a professional clean.

The best time of year to book

In the UAE, the best time is before the peak summer months. The system starts the hottest period clean, it works less hard when it matters most, and you avoid the busiest booking weeks. A second good moment is straight after a heavy dust storm, if it has left the home and the system dusty.

Can you go longer between cleans?

You can stretch the interval by:

  • cleaning or changing the filter regularly
  • keeping windows and balcony doors closed on dusty days
  • vacuuming and dusting regularly, especially in pet homes
  • fixing drips and leaks around the unit quickly, so moisture doesn't build up

Even so, a yearly check is worthwhile, because the inside of the ducts and the coil aren't visible from the room.
