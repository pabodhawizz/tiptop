Move-Out Cleaning Checklist for Sharjah Tenants (Printable)

4 November 2026 · 4 min read · By the Tip Top team

This move-out cleaning checklist lists everything to clean before you hand back a rented flat or villa in Sharjah, grouped room by room so you can print it and tick items off as you go. It assumes the property is empty, which is the best time to clean. Use it for a DIY clean, or to check a professional clean before the landlord's inspection.

For the bigger picture, including what landlords look for, when to book and how to handle the handover, read our main guide to move-out cleaning in Sharjah.

Before you start

  • Read your tenancy contract for any cleaning requirements.
  • Find your move-in photos or inspection report, if you have one.
  • Make sure all furniture, boxes and rubbish have gone.
  • Check water and electricity are still connected.
  • Gather supplies: microfibre cloths, a mop, a vacuum with a narrow nozzle, a squeegee, a degreaser, a descaler, a pH-neutral cleaner for any marble, gloves and bin bags.

Work top to bottom, room by room. Dust high surfaces first and do floors last, so dirt falls onto areas you haven't cleaned yet. Never mix cleaning products.

Move-out cleaning checklist, room by room

Kitchen

  • Inside and outside all cupboards and drawers, including hinges and handles
  • Shelves and cupboard tops (check for sticky dust)
  • Hob degreased, including knobs and edges
  • Extractor hood degreased, filters washed or cleaned
  • Oven inside and out: racks, trays, door glass and seals
  • Fridge and freezer emptied, cleaned, dried, doors left ajar if switched off
  • Microwave and dishwasher, if they belong to the property
  • Splashback tiles and grout
  • Worktops, including behind and under appliances
  • Sink, taps and drain descaled; plughole cleared
  • Bin area wiped and odour-free

Bathrooms

  • Toilet inside, outside, base, seat hinges and cistern
  • Basin, taps and overflow descaled
  • Shower screen and doors free of limescale and soap marks
  • Bath, including the panel and the edge of the sealant
  • Shower head and hose descaled
  • Wall tiles and grout cleaned; mould spots treated
  • Mirrors and mirror cabinets, inside and out
  • Drains cleared of hair
  • Extractor fan cover dusted
  • Floor, including behind the toilet

Bedrooms

  • Inside wardrobes: rails, shelves, drawers and doors
  • Wardrobe tops dusted
  • Walls spot-cleaned for marks and scuffs
  • Light switches and sockets wiped
  • Doors, frames and handles
  • Skirting boards
  • Floors, including under where the bed stood

Living and dining areas

  • Built-in shelves and TV units, inside and out
  • Walls spot-cleaned, nail holes noted (cleaning won't fix them; check your contract)
  • Light fittings and ceiling fans dusted
  • Switches, sockets and thermostats wiped
  • Doors, frames and skirting
  • Floors cleaned edge to edge, including corners

Windows, balcony and AC

  • Interior glass on all windows and glass doors
  • Frames and sills wiped
  • Sliding tracks vacuumed and wiped (they fill with sand quickly)
  • Insect screens brushed or washed
  • Balcony swept and washed; drain outlet clear
  • Balcony glass and railings wiped
  • AC grilles and vents wiped
  • AC filters cleaned, if your contract asks for it

Entrance, laundry and storage

  • Front door both sides, including the frame and peephole
  • Shoe cupboard inside and out
  • Laundry area: washing machine exterior, detergent drawer and seal, if it belongs to the property
  • Storage cupboards and maid's room, if any
  • Water heater cupboard dusted

Villa extras

  • Staircase: steps, risers and handrail
  • Garage or car port swept
  • Garden paths, patio and outdoor seating area swept
  • Roof terrace and outdoor kitchen, if any
  • External lights and doorbell wiped

If you had pets

  • Hair removed from floors, skirting, AC grilles and window tracks
  • Litter tray area cleaned and sanitised
  • Scratch marks on doors noted
  • Pet-odour treatment for any landlord's carpets or soft furnishings

Final checks before handover

  • Walk through every room in daylight and check from different angles.
  • Run taps and flush toilets to check for smells from drains.
  • Take dated photos of each room.
  • Collect all keys, access cards and remotes.
  • Keep any cleaning invoice with your tenancy documents.
