Cat Grooming in Sharjah: Prices and What a Full Groom Includes

4 November 2026 · 3 min read · By the Tip Top team

Cat grooming in Sharjah is usually priced by the cat's coat and condition rather than at one flat rate, because a matted long-haired Persian takes far more time and care than a short-haired cat that only needs a brush and a claw trim. A full groom typically covers a health check, brushing and de-matting, a bath where needed, drying, claws and ears. Here's what's included, what changes the cost, and how to get an accurate quote.

What a full cat groom includes

  1. Health check. The groomer looks over the coat, skin, eyes, ears, claws and paws, and tells you about anything worth showing your vet, such as lumps, sores, flaky skin or signs of fleas.
  2. Brush-out. Loose undercoat is removed, which reduces shedding and hairballs.
  3. De-matting. Tangles and knots are worked out carefully, because cat skin is thin and tears easily.
  4. Bath, if needed. Many cats never need a bath. It's usually for long-haired or greasy coats, older cats that can't groom themselves well, or after something messy.
  5. Drying. Done gently and thoroughly, so the coat isn't left damp against the skin.
  6. Claw trim. Sharp tips are trimmed, avoiding the quick.
  7. Ear care. The outer ear is checked and gently cleaned.
  8. Hygiene tidy. For long-haired cats, a careful trim around the rear and paws helps keep litter and mess out of the fur.

What affects cat grooming prices in Sharjah

When people search for the cat grooming Sharjah price, they're usually hoping for one figure. In practice, the cost for cat grooming depends on how much work your particular cat needs.

How to get an accurate quote

Give the groomer as much detail as you can when you ask for a price:

  • Your cat's breed or coat type, and age
  • Clear photos of the coat, especially any knots behind the ears, under the legs and on the belly
  • How your cat behaves when handled
  • Any health issues or recent vet treatment
  • Whether you want a bath or a dry groom

Photos help the groomer judge matting before the visit. The final price is confirmed once the groomer has seen the coat.

Badly matted cats: if the coat is matted tight to the skin over large areas, or the skin looks red or sore, see a vet first. Severe matting can be painful to remove and may need veterinary care.

Cats in air-conditioned UAE homes can shed through much of the year, so regular brushing is useful for most coats.

Salon or home grooming for cats?

Many cats find travel more stressful than grooming itself: the carrier, the car journey in the heat, and the smell of other animals at a salon all add up. Grooming at home removes those steps. Our article on whether cat grooming at home is less stressful goes into this in detail.

Preparing for a groom

  • Choose a small, quiet room with the door closed, such as a bathroom.
  • Bring your cat into the room before the groomer arrives.
  • Keep dogs and other pets away.
  • Have your cat's vaccination records to hand.

Afterwards, give your cat a quiet place to rest and a treat. Some cats are a little aloof for an hour or two, which is normal.
