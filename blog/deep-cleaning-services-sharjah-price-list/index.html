Deep Cleaning Prices in Sharjah: A Studio-to-Villa Price Guide

4 November 2026 · 4 min read · By the Tip Top team

For a deep cleaning services Sharjah price list, Tip Top's guide prices are: from around AED 199 for a studio, roughly AED 299–599 for a 1–2 bedroom apartment and AED 999+ for villas. Sofa, mattress, carpet and pet-odour treatment are priced per item. The final price is confirmed after a quick assessment, and this guide explains what moves a quote up or down within those figures, and what takes it above them.

What moves a quote within the range

The spread across the AED 299–599 range for a 1–2 bedroom apartment reflects real differences between homes. These are the main factors.

Number of bedrooms and bathrooms

A one-bedroom flat with one bathroom sits towards the lower end; a two-bedroom flat with two or three bathrooms sits higher. Bathrooms take more time than bedrooms because of descaling, grout and sanitising.

Size and layout

Floor area matters as well as room count. A large two-bedroom with a maid's room, a study, a big balcony or lots of glass takes longer than a compact one.

Condition

This is often the biggest factor. Heavy kitchen grease, thick limescale on shower glass, mould in the bathrooms or months of dust on high surfaces all add time. A home that's cleaned regularly and just needs a reset will be quoted lower than one that hasn't had a thorough clean in a long time.

Furnished or empty

An empty property, before moving in or after moving out, is usually quicker to clean thoroughly because nothing is in the way. A furnished home means working around and under furniture and belongings.

Pets

Pet hair in AC grilles, under furniture and in soft furnishings takes extra time. Odour treatment for sofas, carpets or litter areas is priced per item.

What takes a quote above the guide prices

  • More rooms or a larger home. Apartments of three bedrooms or more, and larger or multi-storey villas, are quoted individually.
  • Post-construction or post-renovation dust. Fine construction dust gets into every surface, track and vent, and needs a more intensive clean.
  • Specialist items. Sofa, mattress, carpet and curtain cleaning are priced per item on top of the deep clean.
  • Separate services. AC duct and coil cleaning, pest control, marble polishing, water tank cleaning and facade cleaning are quoted on their own, based on the property.
  • Exceptional condition. Severe build-up that needs repeated treatments or much longer working time.

Get the right quote first time: send clear photos of the kitchen hob and hood, each bathroom's shower and taps, and any problem areas, and mention pets, extras and whether the home will be furnished on the day.

How much does deep cleaning cost in the UAE?

Prices vary between companies and depend on the same factors: size, condition and what's included. Rather than comparing headline numbers, compare like for like:

  • Is it a fixed price for a written scope, or an hourly rate?
  • Does the team bring its own equipment and products?
  • Are inside cupboards, AC grilles, window tracks and balconies included?
  • Is the price confirmed before work starts, or can it change on the day?
  • What happens if something is missed?

A low price for a vague scope can end up costing more if it leaves the bathrooms half done. For a breakdown of what's included in a full visit, see our guide to house cleaning services in Sharjah.

Getting value from a deep clean

  • Time it well. Before moving in, after moving out, after a dusty spell or before a busy season with guests.
  • Declutter first. Clear worktops and surfaces so the team's time goes on cleaning.
  • Combine extras. Adding sofa or mattress cleaning to the same visit saves arranging a second one.
  • Keep it up. Regular upkeep between deep cleans keeps the next one quicker. Our guide on how often to deep clean in the UAE can help you plan.
