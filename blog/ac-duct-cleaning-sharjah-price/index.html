AC Duct Cleaning Price in Sharjah: What Affects the Cost

4 November 2026 · 3 min read · By the Tip Top team

The AC duct cleaning price in Sharjah depends on your system and your property: how many indoor units and vents there are, whether you live in an apartment or a villa, how dirty the ducts are, whether there's mould and how easy the units are to reach. Instead of a one-size price list, the most accurate way to get a figure is to share a few details and photos, or have the system assessed, before any work is booked.

Why a Sharjah price list can mislead

Price lists are handy for comparing, but AC systems vary so much that a fixed list rarely matches what's actually in your home. A "2-bedroom apartment" in one Sharjah building might have a single fan coil unit and a handful of grilles; in another it might have two units and twice as many vents. A villa in Al Zahia or Tilal City could have several ducted systems across two floors. A list that ignores those differences either overcharges small systems or leads to extra charges on larger ones.

Ask instead for a price based on your system, and for the scope to be written down.

What affects the AC duct cleaning price in Sharjah homes

Apartments

  • Number of fan coil units. Many apartments have one unit above a false ceiling; larger ones may have two or more.
  • Number of grilles. Each room usually has at least one supply grille, and there may be separate return grilles.
  • Access hatch. A large, easy hatch is quicker to work through than a small or awkward one.
  • Building rules. Some towers require contractor passes, service-lift bookings or work within set hours, which affects scheduling.

Villas and townhouses

  • Number of systems. Villas often have separate units for different floors or zones.
  • Duct length and layout across larger floor areas and multiple storeys.
  • Roof or loft access to the indoor units.
  • Outdoor units, which collect sand on their coils and may be cleaned as part of the job.

Condition and mould

A system cleaned last year is a lighter job than one with years of dust or renovation debris. Visible mould or a strong musty smell means extra cleaning and sanitising. See our complete guide to AC duct cleaning for what a full clean involves.

What's included

Inside the ducts, grilles, filter, coil, drain tray, sanitising and outdoor units can all be included or quoted separately. Make sure you know which.

Information to send for a quick, accurate quote

If you'd like a photo-based assessment, these details make the quote more accurate:

  1. Property type and area, for example a 2-bedroom apartment in Al Nahda or a 4-bedroom villa in Muwaileh
  2. Number of indoor units, if you know it
  3. Number of grilles or diffusers in each room
  4. A photo of a typical grille, close up
  5. A photo of the access hatch or the unit, if you can reach it safely
  6. Any smells, visible mould, drips or weak airflow
  7. When the system was last cleaned, if you know
  8. Any building rules for contractors

Count the grilles before you call. Walk through each room and note the number of ceiling and wall vents. It's the single detail that most helps a company give you an accurate price over WhatsApp.

Questions that affect what you pay

  • Is the price per unit, per vent or for the whole property?
  • Does it include cleaning inside the ducts with extraction, or only the grilles?
  • Are the coil and drain tray included?
  • Is mould treatment included if it's found, or quoted separately?
  • Are outdoor units included?
  • Could the price change on the day, and if so, why?

Timing and value

Booking before the peak summer months means the system starts the hot season clean, and you're more likely to get your preferred date. Regular cleaning also tends to keep each visit lighter.
