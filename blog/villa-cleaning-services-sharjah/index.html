Villa Cleaning in Sharjah: How a Whole-Villa Deep Clean Is Planned

4 November 2026 · 4 min read · By the Tip Top team

A whole-villa deep clean in Sharjah is planned around three things: the size and layout of the villa, the condition it's in, and who lives there. The best villa cleaning services in Sharjah assess the property first, size the team to finish in one visit, and work to a written room-by-room plan that's checked before handover. Here's how that planning works and what you can do to help it go smoothly.

Why villa cleaning services start with a plan

A villa isn't just a large apartment. There are more bathrooms, often a maid's room and a laundry, stairs and landings, a majlis or formal sitting room that's rarely used, an outdoor kitchen or barbecue area, a garden, a driveway and a roof. Sand blows in through every door and window, and AC grilles in high ceilings collect dust that's hard to reach. Without a plan, teams end up cleaning the obvious rooms well and running out of time for the rest.

Step 1: the assessment

Before any quote, a good villa cleaning service needs to understand the job. That can be an on-site visit or a set of photos and a few questions:

  • How many bedrooms, bathrooms and floors?
  • Is it furnished or empty (for example before move-in or after a renovation)?
  • What's the condition: regular upkeep, heavy grease and limescale, or construction dust?
  • Which outdoor areas are included: terraces, balconies, the outdoor kitchen, the driveway?
  • Are there children, pets or anyone with allergies or asthma?
  • Any extras: sofa, mattress, carpet or curtain cleaning, or pet-odour treatment?

Step 2: sizing the team

A villa deep clean done over several days means living around buckets and wet floors for most of a week. It's usually better to send a larger team and finish in one visit. The team size depends on the villa's size, its condition and how many specialist tasks are included. Larger villas can still take most of a day. Tip Top sizes its teams to the property so the job is done in one visit.

Step 3: the order of work

Professional teams clean from top to bottom and from the inside out, so dust and dirt fall onto areas that haven't been cleaned yet rather than onto finished ones. A typical sequence:

  1. High-level dusting first: ceiling fans, light fittings, AC grilles, the tops of wardrobes and curtain rails.
  2. Upper floor before lower floor, and stairs last, so nobody walks dirt over a finished floor.
  3. Kitchens and bathrooms get the most time: degreasing, descaling and sanitising take longer than dusting.
  4. Glass and windows once the dusty work is done.
  5. Floors last in each area.
  6. Outdoor areas such as terraces and balconies, timed for the cooler part of the day where possible.

Upholstery, mattresses and carpets are often done in parallel by a separate team member, so they have time to dry before the day ends.

Step 4: the room-by-room checklist

A written checklist is what turns "clean the villa" into a job that can be checked. Each room has its own list: inside cupboards, behind doors, skirting, switches, glass, fittings and so on. It also stops rooms like the laundry or maid's room being forgotten. For the full list of what each area should include, see our villa cleaning checklist, room by room.

Step 5: products for family homes

Villas are often family homes with young children, pets and gardens. Ask any provider what products they use and whether rooms need airing afterwards. Tip Top's villa deep clean uses low-odour, non-toxic products around children and pets. In pet homes, one team handles both the cleaning and the pets.

Ask about inspection before handover. A good villa clean ends with a walk-through against the checklist, before the team leaves. Tip Top inspects before handover, and if anything is missed, re-cleans it free.

How you can help the day go well

  • Clear surfaces of personal items, paperwork and valuables so the team can clean rather than tidy.
  • Tell the team about delicate surfaces, such as marble, natural stone or antique furniture.
  • Make sure water and power are on, and the AC works, especially in summer.
  • Plan for pets: a quiet room or a pet sitter while the floors dry.
  • Agree access for the roof, storerooms and outdoor areas before the day.

Deep clean or regular cleaning?

A deep clean resets the whole villa: it reaches the places regular cleaning skips. Many families book one before or after a move, after a renovation, before Ramadan or a family event, or once or twice a year, and keep up with lighter regular cleaning in between.
