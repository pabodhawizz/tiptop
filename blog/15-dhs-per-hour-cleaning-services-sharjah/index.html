AED 15-an-Hour Cleaners vs a Fixed-Price Deep Clean: What You Actually Get

4 November 2026 · 4 min read · By the Tip Top team

Many people in Sharjah search for 15 dhs per hour cleaning services, and a low hourly rate can be a good fit for light, regular upkeep. For a deep clean, though, the hourly rate tells you very little: what matters is how many hours the job really takes, what equipment and products are included, and who is responsible for the result. This guide compares hourly cleaning with a fixed-price deep clean so you can work out which suits the job in front of you.

Why people search for 15 dhs per hour cleaning services in Sharjah

"AED 15 per hour" is one of the rates people type into search when they want affordable hourly cleaning services in Sharjah. An hourly rate feels simple and easy to compare. But rates vary between providers and change over time, and a search phrase isn't a price list. Always check the current rate, any minimum booking and any extra charges directly with the provider.

The useful question isn't "what's the cheapest hour?" but "what will this job cost in total, and what will I get for it?"

How hourly cleaning works

With hourly house cleaning service, you pay for time. A cleaner, usually one person, arrives for a set number of hours and works through what you ask. This works well when:

  • the home is already reasonably clean and needs routine upkeep
  • you want help with mixed chores, such as dishes, laundry or tidying
  • you're happy to supply products and equipment and to direct the work
  • you want flexibility from week to week

Things to check before booking: is there a minimum number of hours, is transport included, who provides the cleaning products and equipment, and what happens if the work isn't finished in the time booked?

How a fixed-price deep clean works

A fixed-price clean is priced for the result rather than the time. The company assesses the property, agrees a written scope and quotes a price before work starts. A team sized to the home works through a room-by-room checklist, usually in one visit, with its own equipment and products. The price doesn't go up if the job takes longer than expected.

Comparing the total, not the rate

To compare fairly, estimate the hours honestly. A deep clean involves descaling bathrooms, degreasing the kitchen hood and cabinets, high dusting, wiping AC grilles, cleaning inside cupboards, window tracks, skirting and doors, and floors edge to edge. One person working alone will take much longer than a team, and may need more than one visit to finish.

So work it out like this:

  1. Estimate how many hours one person would need for the full job.
  2. Multiply by the hourly rate, and add any minimum charges or transport.
  3. Add the cost of any products or equipment you'd need to buy.
  4. Compare that total, and what's included, with a fixed quote for the same scope.

Sometimes hourly will come out cheaper, especially for a small, well-kept home. Sometimes a fixed price works out similar or better, because a team with the right equipment finishes in one visit.

Be specific when you compare. Ask every provider to quote for the same list of tasks. "Clean the bathroom" and "descale glass, taps and tiles, scrub grout and sanitise the toilet" are very different jobs.

The jobs where equipment matters

Some tasks are hard to do well in a few hours with household products:

  • Built-up limescale on shower glass and taps, which is common with UAE water. See what goes into bathroom deep cleaning.
  • Kitchen grease on the hood, filters and cabinet fronts. Our guide to kitchen deep cleaning explains what's involved.
  • Sofa, mattress and carpet cleaning, which need extraction equipment.
  • Fine sand and dust in window tracks, AC grilles and high surfaces.

A sensible way to use both

Hourly help and fixed-price deep cleaning aren't rivals. Many homes benefit from both: a deep clean to reset the home, then regular hourly or scheduled upkeep to keep it there. Upkeep is quicker and cheaper when it starts from a clean baseline. For more on what a professional visit covers, read our guide to house cleaning services in Sharjah.
