How Much Does AC Duct Cleaning Cost in the UAE?

4 November 2026 · 4 min read · By the Tip Top team

AC duct cleaning cost in the UAE depends on how many indoor units and vents you have, the size and type of property, how dirty the system is, whether mould treatment is needed and how easy the ducts are to reach. That's why reliable companies quote after seeing the system, or at least photos of it, rather than giving one fixed figure. The most useful thing you can do is understand how quotes are built, so you can compare them fairly.

Why there's no single "average cost for AC duct cleaning"

If you search for an average price, you'll find figures that vary a lot. That isn't necessarily because some companies overcharge. It's because "AC duct cleaning" can describe very different jobs:

  • a quick wipe of the grilles and a filter clean
  • grilles, filter and coil, with nothing done inside the ducts
  • a full clean with extraction inside every duct, plus coil, drain tray and grilles
  • all of the above, with mould treatment and sanitising

Two prices for "duct cleaning" can be far apart and both be fair, because they're for different work. A low price is only good value if it covers what your system actually needs.

What affects AC duct cleaning cost

Number of indoor units

Each indoor unit has its own coil, filter and drain tray to clean, and usually its own set of ducts. A home with one fan coil unit is a smaller job than a villa with several ducted systems.

Number of vents and grilles

Every grille has to be removed, washed and refitted, and each duct branch has to be cleaned. More vents means more time.

Property size and type

Studios and smaller apartments usually have short duct runs. Larger apartments, townhouses and multi-storey villas tend to have longer, more complex ductwork.

Condition of the system

A system that's cleaned every year is quicker to clean than one that hasn't been touched in years, or one full of renovation dust.

Mould

Visible mould or a strong musty smell means extra cleaning and sanitising of the coil, drain tray and affected ducts. Our article on mould in your AC explains what's involved.

Access

Units behind small ceiling hatches, in tight roof spaces or high up in double-height rooms take longer to reach safely. In towers, building access rules can also affect timing.

Extras

Outdoor condenser coil cleaning, filter replacement and any repairs found during inspection may be quoted separately.

How AC duct cleaning price quotes are usually structured

Quote typeHow it worksWhat to check
Per unitA price for each indoor unit and its ductsHow many vents are included per unit
Per vent or grilleA price for each outlet cleanedWhether the unit, coil and drain tray are included
Whole-property packageOne price for an apartment or villaWhat's excluded, and what happens if the system is dirtier than expected
Itemised after inspectionA breakdown based on what was seenThat every line matches the work you want

Any of these can be fair. What matters is that the scope is clear and written down before work starts.

How to compare quotes

  1. Give every company the same information: number of units, number of vents, property type, any smells or visible mould, and photos.
  2. Ask what's included: inside the ducts, grilles, filter, coil, drain tray, sanitising, outdoor units.
  3. Ask about equipment. A thorough job uses extraction, not just brushes.
  4. Ask what could change the price on the day, and how you'll be told before any extra work.
  5. Ask for before-and-after photos as part of the job.

Be wary of very low fixed prices given without any questions. They often cover the grilles and filter only, or lead to add-ons once the team is in your home. A price based on your actual system is more likely to match the work done.

Ways to keep the cost reasonable

  • Clean the filter regularly. Less dust gets into the system, so the next clean is easier.
  • Book before the hottest months, before the system has had a full summer of build-up.
  • Combine duct and coil cleaning in one visit, rather than booking them separately.
  • Deal with leaks and drips early, before they lead to mould.

Is the cost worth it?

For most UAE homes with the AC running much of the year, an annual duct and coil clean is good value when it's done properly and solves a real problem, such as dust from the vents or a musty smell.
