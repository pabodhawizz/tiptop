Post-Construction Cleaning Checklist: Dust, Paint and Grout

4 November 2026 · 3 min read · By the Tip Top team

A post-construction cleaning checklist helps you clean, or check a cleaning team's work, after building or renovation work, so nothing is missed before you move in. The three big jobs are removing fine dust from every surface, getting paint specks and overspray off glass and fittings, and clearing grout and cement haze from tiles. This checklist is grouped by stage and by room, so you can use it as a template for a DIY clean or for your handover walk-through.

Stage 1: before cleaning starts

  • All trades finished, including snagging fixes and paint touch-ups
  • Builders' waste, offcuts and packaging removed by the contractor
  • Water and electricity working
  • Windows opened for ventilation; dust masks and gloves ready if cleaning yourself
  • Protective films still on? Note which ones need removing
  • Floor types noted: marble, porcelain, wood, so the right products are used

Test before you treat. Try every product and tool on a small, hidden area first. Acidic grout-haze removers can etch marble and natural stone, and the wrong scraper will scratch glass.

Stage 2: dust removal, top to bottom

  • Ceilings, cornices and corners dusted
  • Light fittings, spotlights and ceiling fans dusted (power off)
  • AC grilles and vents vacuumed and wiped
  • Tops of doors, frames, wardrobes and high shelves
  • Walls dusted or lightly wiped
  • Switches and sockets wiped with a barely damp cloth (power off)
  • Inside every cupboard, drawer and wardrobe vacuumed, then wiped
  • Window sills and sliding tracks vacuumed
  • Skirting boards vacuumed and wiped
  • Floors vacuumed thoroughly before any wet mopping

Stage 3: paint, adhesive and film removal

  • Paint specks and overspray removed from window glass
  • Paint removed from window and door frames, handles and hinges
  • Paint drips removed from floors and skirting
  • Paint marks cleaned from switches, sockets and fittings
  • Protective films peeled from glass, frames and appliances
  • Sticker and label residue removed from sanitary ware, glass and appliances
  • Silicone smears cleaned from tiles, basins, baths and shower trays
  • Adhesive and tape marks removed from floors and worktops

Stage 4: grout and tile haze

  • Grout haze cleaned from floor tiles
  • Grout haze cleaned from wall tiles and splashbacks
  • Cement or mortar spots removed from tiles and thresholds
  • Grout lines cleaned and checked for gaps
  • Marble and natural stone cleaned only with pH-neutral or stone-safe products

Stage 5: room-by-room detail

Kitchen

  • Cabinets inside and out, including hinges and handles
  • Worktops, splashback and sink
  • Hob, hood and oven (inside, if newly installed with packaging dust)
  • Fridge, dishwasher and other appliances inside and out
  • Plinths and the floor under appliances

Bathrooms

  • Toilet, basin, bath and shower cleaned and sanitised
  • Shower glass free of haze, silicone and stickers
  • Taps, shower heads and fittings polished
  • Mirrors and cabinets inside and out
  • Drains checked and cleared of debris
  • Extractor fan cover dusted

Bedrooms and living areas

  • Wardrobes and built-in units inside and out
  • Doors, frames and handles
  • Floors cleaned edge to edge

Windows, balconies and outdoors

  • Glass inside and, where safely reachable, outside
  • Frames, seals, sills and tracks
  • Balcony floor, glass and railings; drain outlet clear
  • For villas: terraces, steps, entrance and garage swept and washed

Stage 6: final pass

  • Wait a few hours, or until the next day, for airborne dust to settle
  • Wipe all horizontal surfaces again
  • Final vacuum and mop of all floors
  • Check AC filters; clean or replace if dusty
  • Run taps and flush toilets to clear the pipes

Handover walk-through checks

  • Look at glass in daylight and from an angle for streaks and paint specks
  • Run a finger along door tops, shelves and frames
  • Open every cupboard and drawer
  • Check corners and floor edges, not just the middle of the room
  • Check tiles from a low angle for haze
  • Photograph anything that needs another pass
