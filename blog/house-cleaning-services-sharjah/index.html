House Cleaning Services in Sharjah: What a Professional Visit Includes

4 November 2026 · 3 min read · By the Tip Top team

Booking a house cleaning service in Sharjah should be simple. In practice, "cleaning" can mean anything from a quick tidy to a full top-to-bottom clean, and the price and the result vary just as much. This guide explains what a professional visit should include, so you know what you're paying for before the team arrives.

Regular cleaning vs deep cleaning

Most house cleaning services in Sharjah offer two broad levels:

  • Regular cleaning keeps an already clean home in good shape: dusting, vacuuming, mopping, wiping kitchen and bathroom surfaces, emptying bins.
  • Deep cleaning resets a home: descaling, degreasing, cleaning inside and behind things, and the details that a regular clean skips, such as skirting, switches, doors and cabinet fronts.

If your home hasn't had a thorough clean in months, or you're moving in or out, start with a deep clean. Regular visits are much easier, and cheaper, once the home is already at a good baseline.

What a professional deep clean covers, room by room

Kitchen

  • Countertops, splashback and sink descaled and sanitised
  • Cabinet fronts and handles cleaned
  • Hob and extractor hood degreased
  • Outside of appliances wiped (ask in advance if you want the inside of the oven or fridge done)
  • Floor cleaned in detail, including edges and under movable items

Bathrooms

  • Limescale removed from taps, shower screens, tiles and fittings
  • Toilet, basin and shower or bath sanitised
  • Grout and corners scrubbed
  • Mirrors and glass polished

Living areas and bedrooms

  • Dusting throughout, including high surfaces and skirting
  • Interior windows, doors, frames and switches wiped
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped in detail
  • Furniture surfaces cleaned

Sofa, mattress and carpet cleaning are usually separate add-ons because they need extraction equipment, not just cloths and a mop.

How long a visit takes

Most apartments take about 3–6 hours for a deep clean, depending on size and condition. Larger villas can take most of a day. A good company sends a team sized to your property, so the job is finished in one visit rather than spread over several days.

What house cleaning costs in Sharjah

As a guide, Tip Top's deep cleaning starts from around AED 199 for a studio, roughly AED 299–599 for a 1–2 bedroom apartment, and AED 999+ for villas. Sofa, mattress, carpet and pet-odour treatment are priced per item. The final price is confirmed after a quick assessment, so there are no surprises on the day.

When you compare quotes, check that you're comparing the same thing. An hourly rate looks cheaper until you see how many hours it takes and what's not included. A fixed price for a defined checklist is easier to hold a company to.

Products: what's safe around children and pets

Many Sharjah homes have small children, pets, or both. Ask what products the team brings, whether they're low-odour and non-toxic, and whether anything needs time to dry before children or animals go back into a room. At Tip Top, low-odour, non-toxic products are the default, not an upgrade.

How to get the most from your visit

  1. Declutter surfaces the night before, so the team spends its time cleaning rather than moving things.
  2. Point out priorities at the start: a stained grout line, a greasy hood, a room guests will use.
  3. Secure pets and valuables, or tell the team where they are.
  4. Walk through the checklist at the end, before the team leaves.
