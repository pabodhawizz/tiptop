Pet grooming at home in Sharjah means a groomer comes to your villa or apartment, sets up in a bathroom or utility area, and grooms your dog, cat or small animal without a trip to a salon. A typical visit runs from a quick health look-over through brushing, bathing, drying, nails and ears, then a tidy-up of the space. Here's what happens at each stage, so you know what to expect and how to prepare.

Before the groomer arrives

Booking and information

When you book, the groomer will usually ask about your animal: species, breed or type, size, coat, age and temperament. This helps them plan the time needed and bring the right tools. Mention any health issues, skin sensitivities, recent surgery or previous bad experiences with grooming.

Choosing the space

Pick a room with good light, a non-slip floor and easy access to water. A bathroom with a shower works well for most dogs; a smaller space with a basin or sink suits cats and small animals. Clear away anything that might get wet or knocked over.

Settling your pet

Take your dog for a short walk at a cool time of day before the visit, so it's calmer and has been to the toilet. Keep other pets in a separate room.

Pet grooming at home, step by step

  1. Greeting and health check. The groomer lets your pet get used to them, then looks over the coat, skin, ears, eyes, paws and nails, and points out anything worth showing your vet.
  2. Brush-out and de-matting. Loose hair and tangles are worked out before washing, because water tightens mats.
  3. Bath. A shampoo suited to the animal and coat, worked through to the skin and rinsed thoroughly. Not every animal needs a bath every visit; many cats and small animals are groomed mostly dry.
  4. Drying. The coat is dried and brushed through so it isn't left damp against the skin, which matters in humid weather.
  5. Nail trim. Nails are trimmed to a comfortable length, avoiding the quick.
  6. Ear care. Ears are checked and gently cleaned.
  7. Finishing. A final brush, and a tidy trim where the breed and coat need one.
  8. Clean-up and handover. Hair and water are cleared from the work area, and you get a quick summary of anything the groomer noticed.

CatsBrushing and de-matting, nails and ears; bathing only when needed, as most cats keep themselves clean
Rabbits and other small animalsGentle brushing, nail trims and checking the coat; bathing is generally avoided

Why owners in Sharjah choose home grooming

  • No car journey in the heat, which matters for much of the year.
  • A calmer pet. Many animals are more relaxed without other pets, noise and a strange place.
  • One-to-one attention from start to finish.
  • Convenience, especially for owners without a car or with several pets.

Looking for affordable pet grooming in Sharjah?

Many people search for cheap pet grooming, but the better question is what you're getting for the price. Grooming is usually priced by animal and coat, because a large double-coated dog takes far longer than a small short-haired one. When comparing quotes, check:

  • Exactly what's included: bath, brush, nails, ears, trim
  • Whether de-matting or a heavy de-shed costs extra
  • How long the groomer plans to spend with your pet
  • What products are used

Keeping up with brushing between visits is the easiest way to keep each groom quicker and simpler, since a well-kept coat needs less work.

After the groom

Expect your pet to be a little tired, and give it a quiet spot to rest. If the groomer mentioned anything about the skin, ears or paws, keep an eye on it and see your vet if it doesn't settle.
