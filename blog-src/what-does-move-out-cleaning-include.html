What does move-out cleaning include? A professional move-out clean usually covers the inside and outside of all cupboards and wardrobes, a full kitchen degrease, descaled and sanitised bathrooms, interior windows and tracks, wiped walls, doors and switches, and detailed floor cleaning. What it often doesn't include by default are exterior windows on upper floors, appliance interiors such as the oven or fridge on some quotes, soft furnishings, and anything that's really repair work, such as filling holes or repainting. The simplest way to avoid surprises is to ask for the scope in writing before you book.

What does move-out cleaning include, area by area?

Every company's list is slightly different, but a full move-out clean should cover these as standard.

Walls, doors and fixtures

  • Walls spot-cleaned for fingerprints, marks and light scuffs
  • Doors, frames and handles wiped on both sides
  • Light switches, sockets and thermostats wiped
  • Skirting boards dusted and wiped

Spot-cleaning is not the same as washing every wall from floor to ceiling. If walls are heavily marked, ask whether full wall washing is included or priced separately.

Kitchen and appliances

  • Cupboards and drawers inside and out
  • Hob, extractor hood and splashback degreased
  • Sink and taps descaled
  • Appliance exteriors wiped

Oven, fridge and freezer interiors are often included in a move-out clean, because landlords check them, but confirm it. An oven with heavy baked-on grease takes much longer than one that's been looked after.

Bathrooms

  • Toilets, basins, showers and baths cleaned and sanitised
  • Limescale removed from taps, shower heads and glass
  • Tiles and grout cleaned
  • Mirrors and cabinets inside and out

Bedrooms and living areas

  • Inside wardrobes and built-in units
  • Shelves, ledges and light fittings dusted
  • Floors cleaned edge to edge, including corners

Balcony and AC grilles

  • Balcony swept and washed
  • AC grilles wiped

Does move-out cleaning include windows?

Usually, yes, but with a distinction:

  • Interior glass, frames, sills and sliding tracks are normally included. In Sharjah, the tracks matter: they fill with fine sand, and a landlord will notice.
  • The balcony side of sliding doors is normally included where it can be reached safely.
  • Exterior glass on upper floors that can only be reached from outside is usually not included, for safety reasons. In apartment buildings, that glass is often cleaned by the building itself.

For villas, ask specifically about upper-floor and stairwell windows, and whether outside glass is part of the job.

What cleaning can't fix

Cleaning isn't repair. A move-out clean removes dirt, grease and limescale. It won't fill nail holes, repaint walls, fix broken fittings, remove permanent stains in grout or reverse burns and scratches. If your contract requires repainting or repairs, arrange those separately and before the clean.

What's not included because it shouldn't be there

A cleaning team expects an empty home. Most will not:

  • pack or move your belongings and furniture
  • dispose of large items, furniture or bulky rubbish
  • clean around boxes that are still in the property

Book the clean for after the movers have gone, so nothing blocks access.
