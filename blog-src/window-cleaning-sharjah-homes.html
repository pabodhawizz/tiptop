In Sharjah, windows get dirty faster than almost anywhere: fine sand settles on the glass within days, humidity turns that dust into a film, and the water used to wash them often leaves white spots behind. Good window cleaning deals with all three, which means removing grit before scrubbing, using the right water and technique, and not forgetting the frames and tracks where sand collects.

Why windows in Sharjah get dirty so quickly

Most homes here face the same three problems, and each one needs a slightly different approach.

Fine dust and sand

Wind-blown sand is fine enough to settle into every corner of a window. On the glass it looks like a dull haze; in the sliding tracks it builds into a gritty layer that makes doors stiff and wears the rollers. Wiping dusty glass with a dry cloth drags those particles across the surface and can leave fine scratches over time.

Humidity and salt

On humid mornings, condensation forms on the outside of cool, air-conditioned glass. It mixes with the dust and dries as streaks. Homes near the coast, such as Al Khan, Al Majaz Waterfront or Maryam Island, can also pick up a salty film from sea air, which leaves glass looking cloudy even after a quick wipe.

Hard-water spots

Those small white rings on glass are usually mineral deposits left when water evaporates. They come from sprinklers, balcony washing, AC drips from the unit above, or from cleaning with tap water and leaving it to dry. Once they've baked on in the sun, a normal glass spray often won't shift them.

What a thorough window cleaning job includes

A proper clean is more than the glass. For each window or glass door, expect:

  • Dry removal of loose dust and sand from glass, frames and sills before any water goes on
  • Glass washed and squeegeed, inside and, where it can be reached safely, outside
  • Frames and sills wiped, including the rubber seals
  • Sliding tracks vacuumed and cleaned, so doors run smoothly again
  • Mesh insect screens brushed or washed, if fitted
  • Edges and corners detailed and the glass dried so it doesn't streak or spot

Balcony glass balustrades and shower screens often need the same treatment, and they're usually where hard-water marks are worst.

Safety first. Never lean out of a window or stand on a balcony railing to reach the outside of the glass. Outer panes on upper floors that can't be reached safely from inside need proper access equipment, or should be left to the building's facade cleaning.

DIY window cleaning tips that actually work

For regular upkeep between professional cleans, a simple routine goes a long way:

  1. Vacuum the tracks first with a narrow nozzle, then wipe them with a damp cloth.
  2. Brush or dry-dust the glass to lift sand before you wet it.
  3. Use a mild solution: a few drops of washing-up liquid in warm water works well. Don't mix different cleaning products.
  4. Squeegee from top to bottom, wiping the blade after each stroke.
  5. Dry the edges with a lint-free microfibre cloth so drips don't leave spots.
  6. Clean in the shade, early morning or evening. In direct sun the glass dries before you finish and streaks.

For mild hard-water marks, try a dedicated glass descaler suitable for your frames, tested on a small area first. Keep acidic products away from marble sills, aluminium finishes and natural stone, which they can damage. How often should windows be cleaned?

AreaSuggested routine
Inside glassEvery few weeks, or when smudges show
Sliding tracksMonthly, and after any dust storm
Outside glass you can reach safelyEvery one to three months
Full professional clean, inside and outA few times a year, and before or after a move

Homes near construction sites, main roads or the sea usually need a shorter cycle. After a sandstorm, wait until the air has cleared before cleaning, or you'll be doing it twice.

Apartments vs villas

In an apartment, you can normally clean the inside glass and the balcony side of sliding doors yourself. Fixed outer panes on high floors are usually the building's responsibility, handled as part of its exterior cleaning. Ask your building management how often that happens.

In a villa or townhouse, all the glass is yours to look after, including skylights, upper-floor windows and glass over the stairwell. Higher windows need the right ladders or access equipment and someone trained to use them. What affects the cost of a window cleaning service

There's no single price for window cleaning, because the work varies so much. The main factors are:

  • the number and size of windows and glass doors
  • whether the outside glass is included, and how it can be reached
  • the height of the windows and any access equipment needed
  • how much hard-water staining or build-up there is
  • whether tracks, screens and balcony glass are included
